Integrating Payment Gateways & Shipping Logistics in Kerala

Building an online store is easy, but managing payment approvals and package deliveries is where most local business owners get stuck. Techora takes care of these technical hurdles directly.

Razorpay & Cashfree API

We link your store directly with trusted Indian payment gateways like Razorpay, Cashfree, or Instamojo. We guide you through the KYC approval process—whether you have a registered GST certificate or just a basic sole proprietorship bank account.

India Post & Courier Integration

We connect your admin panel to shipping platforms like Shiprocket, Delhivery, or DTDC. More importantly, we integrate India Post API, ensuring you can reliably ship orders to deep rural pincodes across Kerala where private couriers do not deliver.

WhatsApp Tracking Alerts

Skip expensive SMS gateways. We configure automated WhatsApp notifications. Whenever an order is placed, packaged, or shipped, the system automatically sends a direct WhatsApp update to the customer with their tracking link.

Specific, clean codebases: We don't build bloated stores using heavy plugins that crash. We deliver clean, fast, static-first frontends that load in under 2 seconds on mobile networks in interior rural regions, ensuring you don't lose sales due to loading lag.
