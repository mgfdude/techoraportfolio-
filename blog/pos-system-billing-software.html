Choosing POS Hardware & Operating Offline in Kerala

A beautiful billing interface is useless if it stops working when your internet drops. Network fluctuations are extremely common across Kerala—especially during monsoon seasons in semi-urban areas like Mannarkkad, Perinthalmanna, or Ottapalam.

100% Offline Billing

Our POS billing systems operate entirely offline. All transactions, sales, and bills are saved in a highly secure local database. The moment your internet is restored, the data automatically syncs to your cloud dashboard without losing a single transaction.

Hardware Compatibility

You don't need expensive proprietary terminals. Our system runs perfectly on any standard Windows PC, laptop, or Android tablet. It integrates seamlessly with budget-friendly 58mm/80mm thermal printers (Epson, TVS, Pegasus) and USB barcode scanners.

GST & Cess Ready

Easily print clean invoices with structured CGST, SGST, and any applicable local taxes. We design customizable bill receipt formats for A4 invoices, 3-inch table receipts, and 2-inch retail slips, matching your specific shop size.

POS System FAQ

Answers to common questions about our billing software solutions.

What is a POS system?

A POS (Point of Sale) system is software used to manage sales, inventory, and customer data. It replaces traditional cash registers with a smart, digital interface.

How much does POS software cost in Kerala?

Pricing depends on your requirements (number of devices, features like cloud sync, etc.). We offer highly competitive rates for Kerala startups and established shops.

Is cloud POS better for restaurants?

Yes. Cloud-based POS systems like Techora's allow you to track your kitchen, inventory, and sales from anywhere, even when you aren't at the restaurant.

Can POS software manage inventory?

Yes, our system automatically deducts items from stock when a sale is made and provides reports on low-stock items.

Does Techora provide restaurant billing systems?

Yes, we build complete restaurant management systems including billing, KDS, and WhatsApp-integrated ordering.
