    What is a Kitchen Display System (KDS)?

    A Kitchen Display System (KDS) is a digital order management screen used in restaurant kitchens to replace traditional paper tickets or KOTs. When an order is placed through the POS or a QR Menu, it instantly appears on the KDS screen in the kitchen.

    For modern restaurants in Kerala, a KDS is the ultimate tool to bridge the gap between waiters and chefs, ensuring that orders are prepared correctly and served on time.

    Problems in Traditional Paper-Based Kitchens

    Manual order management is prone to errors that can cost your restaurant time, money, and reputation:

    Paper Tickets

    KOTs get lost, stained, or become unreadable in a busy, greasy kitchen environment.

    Missed Orders

    Chefs might miss a ticket during peak hours, leading to long wait times and angry customers.

    Duplicate Items

    Confusion between staff can lead to double-cooking the same dish, causing food waste.

    Delayed Serving

    Without order tracking, it's hard to know which table has been waiting the longest.

    Local Intranet Setup: Running KDS Without Internet Interruption

    A kitchen system that lags or drops during a busy dinner rush is worse than using paper tickets. Since active internet connections are not 100% stable in Kerala, we do not rely on active broadband for kitchen communications.

    Local Intranet Server

    Our KDS is configured to run on a local intranet server. The POS terminal and kitchen display screen communicate directly through your local Wi-Fi router. It works in milliseconds even if the outside internet is completely disconnected.

    Color-Coded Timers

    Orders are automatically sorted and color-coded on the screen based on their age. New orders appear neutral, turn yellow after 8 minutes, and flash red after 12 minutes to keep the cooking crew focused and prevent cold serving.

    Standard Android Hardware

    You can buy standard 10-inch Android tablets (Lenovo, Samsung) or mount standard LED TVs inside the kitchen using budget-friendly Android boxes. We handle the brackets mounting and network configuration on-site.
